## What the page answers, top to bottom

1. **The answer.** One lean and one probability ("Leaning up, 61%"), read off
   the forecast's chance of finishing above the reference price at the end of
   the horizon, and the two prices that decide it ("Above A the call holds,
   below B it flips"). A is the nearest tested support or resistance when one
   sits inside three quarters of the 68% range, the forecast's own range
   otherwise. No buy or sell wording anywhere: the public page is not a
   SEBI research service.
2. **The chart, NOW in the middle** (`C.ui.centreNow`, `chart.js frameView`).
   Left half: candles with the trail (what the forecast said one lead earlier)
   and the frozen call over them. Right half: the projection to the bell, or
   the whole next session once the market has shut.
3. **What is moving the line.** Every live lane, strongest first, one row each.
4. **AI desk, Said vs did, What it has learned.** See below.
5. **Everything behind the call,** folded. Every Classic panel is still on the
   page (app.js writes to all of them), inside `<details>` the page remembers.

A badge says "Prices from <time>" when the candles are not today's: the live
feed goes through a public proxy, and the saved copy it falls back to must
never read as a live call.

## The AI desk (`assets/js/desk.js`)

TradingAgents (TauricResearch, Apache 2.0) cut to the free tier and pointed at
India. Six requests a run, seven when the sentiment analyst has data:

| Step | Requests | Reads |
|---|---|---|
| Technical analyst | 1 | candles, indicators, levels, patterns with their measured record |
| News analyst | 1 | headlines naming the company first (stocks) or the market's (index); impact, not the lexicon's sentiment |
| Flows and macro analyst | 1 | global cues, GIFT Nifty, India VIX, FII/DII, FII futures book, NIFTY options |
| Company analyst (stock) or market internals (index) | 1 | valuation and street targets from the screener, broker calls in the news, NSE event calendar, bulk/block deals, insider filings; or breadth, sectors, index movers |
| Sentiment analyst, when its sources answer (30 Oct 2026) | 1 | Tickertape's Market Mood Index (now, a day, a week and a month ago) and the symbol's StockTwits stream (`NIFTY50.NSE`, `SYMBOL.NSE`; SENSEX has none), promotions dropped (`data.getSentiment`). Reddit, which TradingAgents reads, answers 403 to every proxy |
| Bull vs bear debate | 1 | the analysts' reports |
| Final call and risk check | 1 | reports, debate, and up to four lessons from similar past situations |

The steps run one after another, 800 ms apart. A person's chosen free model
is often held to about one request at a time upstream, and four at once lost
two of six calls on the first live run. A step that fails for a reason that
passes is asked once more: busy or down, 4 s later, naming the free pool
behind the chosen model (`models: [chosen, 'openrouter/free']`, still one
request); empty or unreadable, on the pool itself, because the router does
not fall back on a 200. Six or seven requests a run when every step answers first time, fourteen at
most. A step that still fails says why on the card.

Rules, each with a selftest:

- **It never sees the engine's verdict.** No direction, confidence, target,
  probability or checkpoint value reaches a prompt; only the horizon's end
  time. `selftest` plants sentinel values and greps every prompt for them.
- **It votes and nothing else.** The final score is the model lane's vote,
  valid until the horizon ends or three hours pass, whichever is first
  (`voteFor`, `modelLane` honours `ttlMs`). The learner moves its weight off
  settled calls like any lane.
- **Replies are parsed, not trusted.** Fences, smart quotes and trailing commas
  are repaired; a score off the -1..1 scale is refused, not clamped; a rating
  contradicting its own score keeps the rating; any sentence that instructs a
  trade is dropped (`noAdvice`, which ends a sentence only at a mark followed
  by a space, so "+1.17%" survives it).
- **Budget.** `data.askModel` counts every request per UTC day (the free limit
  resets 05:30 IST): 50 a day under $10 of credit, 1,000 after. A 429 naming
  the daily limit marks the day spent. Automatic runs are NIFTY only, one per
  part of the day (pre, am, pm, post), at least 75 minutes apart, keeping 8
  requests in reserve for stocks opened by hand. Classic's single-call vote
  and narrative stop at 20 left and do not run on V2 at all.

## Said vs did

One row per session: the call frozen before it opened (`ledger.lock`) against
where it closed. Each locked point is joined to the bar whose span holds it,
not to a bar with the same stamp: a path built while a bar was still forming
carries that bar's trade-second stamp, and three sessions on the first live
check could never have been scored otherwise. A lock is frozen on at least
half its points and a price from its last tenth. A past session with no
prices on file reads "not scored", and the record counts them out loud.

## Lessons (`assets/js/lessons.js`)

TradingAgents' reflection loop. When a desk run's horizon has passed, it is
settled against the close at that time (`desk.settle`), and the AI writes one
sentence about what to remember (`desk.reflect`, one request, arithmetic
fallback when the budget is spent, labelled as such). A lesson is filed under
the situation it was made in: trend (range/up/down), RSI, VIX, the open, news
tone and FII flow, in buckets. The next final call reads the four most similar
lessons, ranked by similarity first and age second (45-day half-life), never
below 0.45 similarity. Stored in this browser; Export and Import on the card.

The lane weights still belong to `learn.js` (bounded, min 20 settled calls a
lane, max 8% a step). Lessons never move a number.

## Any NSE stock (`assets/js/stocks.js`)

Search covers `data/universe.json` (about 2,600 NSE names) plus NIFTY and
SENSEX; index members rank above namesakes. A stock is registered as a symbol
(`yahoo: SYM.NS`, `stock: true`) and the same engine runs on it:

- price action lanes read the stock's own candles (Yahoo through `r.jina.ai`);
- the news lane reads headlines naming the company (aliases unique to it, so
  "Reliance" alone, which six companies answer to, matches none) plus macro
  stories (`forecast.setFocus`);
- NIFTY's option chain and seasonal study go dark;
- the opening gap is the index model's gap times the stock's beta from the
  screener, clamped 0.3 to 2;
- the quote is TradingView's screener, 15 minutes delayed and labelled so, and
  is never painted onto the live candle; the learner never learns from a stock;
- after the bell the session view forecasts the next session from the close.
  A stock's last 1-minute bar is 15:29 (the index has a 15:30 print), which
  used to read as one minute left (`forecast.sessionOrigin`).

`#s=RELIANCE` links straight to a stock.

## The price model (`scripts/kronos_forecast.py`)

Kronos (shiyu-coder, MIT), a model trained on candles from 45 exchanges, asked
for sixteen paths per series from the last close: NIFTY next session on
5-minute bars, NIFTY and SENSEX next ten sessions on daily bars. It votes the
median path's end against the paths' own spread, as the `kronos` lane (weight
0.0601, below par). Dark when there is no run, the run expired, it read a
different series, or it is a stub.

It runs from `.github/workflows/kronos.yml` (moved there 30 Oct 2026) twice a
weekday (08:10 and 16:10 IST),
in `live-data`'s concurrency group, and publishes through `publish_live.sh`.
Not yet run in Actions as of 29 Oct 2026; the first run is the verification.
