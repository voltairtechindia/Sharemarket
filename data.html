Bulk and block deals —

A bulk deal is one client trading more than 0.5% of a company's shares in a session; a block deal is a large negotiated trade in the separate window. Both are published by NSE with the client's name.

      Who is long index futures —

      NSE's participant-wise open interest, one file per session. The share of each participant's index-futures book that is long; the change since the previous session is what the forecast's flow lane reads.

      Stocks where F&O open interest moved most today. Rising OI with a rising price is new longs; rising OI with a falling price is new shorts.
